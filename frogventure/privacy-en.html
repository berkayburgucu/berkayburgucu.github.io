2. Data the Game itself collects

Analytics does not use the advertising identifier and is not used for ad personalization. Firebase Privacy
  • Apple processes all payments; we never receive your payment details.

5. International transfers

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete the app. Third-party data is kept as described in each provider's policy.
