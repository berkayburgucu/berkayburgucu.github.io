Frogventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Türkçe

1. Who we are

Frogventure ("the Game") is published by Berkay Burgucu (Moonsting Games), İstanbul, Türkiye ("we"). Contact: moonstinggames@gmail.com

2. Data the Game itself collects

None. The Game does not ask for an account, name, email, phone number, location or contacts. Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes them. We use no crash-reporting, analytics or player-tracking software.

3. Ads and purchases

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC, USA) shows rewarded and interstitial ads and may process a device identifier (IDFV), approximate location derived from IP address, device model and OS, ad interactions and crash data. Ads are non-personalized: we do not access your Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not track you across apps and we do not request tracking permission (ATT). Ad content is limited to rating G. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple processes purchases through its own system. Your payment details never reach us; we only see on your device that a purchase completed. Apple Privacy Policy

If you buy "Remove ads", no ads are shown and the app does not connect to AdMob.

4. If you contact us

If you email support we use your address and message only to reply to you, do not share them, and delete them within one year after the matter is closed.

5. If you visit this page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs (such as your IP address) under its own policy; we have no access to them. GitHub Privacy Statement

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; ads are limited to content rated G and the Game has no chat. If you believe a child's data has been collected, contact us.

6b. Purposes and international transfers

Serving ads, preventing fraud and fixing errors. Google's servers are located outside Türkiye, including in the United States, and transfers take place under appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete the app.

8. Security

Game data stays on your device and its security depends on your device (screen lock, backup settings). Communication with the ad service uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under KVKK in Türkiye, GDPR in the EU/UK, or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Write to moonstinggames@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published at this address.
