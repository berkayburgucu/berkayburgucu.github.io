Frogventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Türkçe

1. Who we are

Frogventure ("the Game") is published by Berkay Burgucu (Moonsting Games), İstanbul, Türkiye ("we"). Contact: moonstinggames@gmail.com

2. What data do we collect?

None. The Game does not ask for an account, name, email, phone number, location, contacts or advertising identifier. It contains no third-party software for analytics, advertising or crash reporting and sends no personal data over the internet. Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes them. We therefore do not process your personal data or transfer it abroad.

3. Apple

Downloading the app and making in-app purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's own services and privacy policy. Your payment details never reach us; we only see on your device that a purchase completed. Apple Privacy Policy

4. If you contact us

If you email support we use your address and message only to reply to you, do not share them, and delete them within one year after the matter is closed.

5. If you visit this page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs (such as your IP address) under its own policy; we have no access to them. GitHub Privacy Statement

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience. Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children; the Game has no ads or chat.

7. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete the app.

8. Security

Because game data never leaves your device, its security depends on your device (screen lock, backup settings).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under KVKK in Türkiye, GDPR in the EU/UK, or CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Write to moonstinggames@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published at this address.
